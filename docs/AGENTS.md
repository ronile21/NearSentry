# Documentation Scope Instructions

Applies to everything under `docs/`.

Primary owner: Documentation Agent.

Rules:
- product intent belongs in `00-product/`
- system/component design belongs in `01-design/`
- normative behavior belongs in `02-specifications/`
- architectural decisions belong in `03-decisions/`
- verification strategy/evidence belongs in `04-testing/`
- permanent version history belongs in `05-versions/`
- operational procedures belong in `06-operations/`
- accepted ADRs/version records are historical artifacts; supersede, do not rewrite history
